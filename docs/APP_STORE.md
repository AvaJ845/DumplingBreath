## 1. Product name rationale

**"Dumpling Breath"** is the ownable mark. It is:

- **A character, not a keyword.** The ROAST is explicit — a trend keyword
  ("Squishy Dumpling Fidget…") has the shortest half-life in the store and
  editors write about original things, not SEO strings. One named dumpling is a
  thing a parent or an editor forwards.
- **Concrete and warm.** "Dumpling" carries the whole promise: something small,
  soft, plump, and a little bit funny that you hold in your hand. It disarms the
  clinical dread of the word "breathing app".
- **Memorable and sayable** to Siri ("start Dumpling Breath") and in a Today-tab
  headline.
- **Not "mindfulness", "calm", or "zen"** — those shelves are owned by
  Calm/Headspace/Apple Mindfulness and an indie brand there is invisible.

Trade-off we are accepting (per playbook §1): an unknown indie normally should
not spend the 30-char Name field on brand alone. We split the difference — the
brand leads (editor-clean, ownable) and a short keyword tail still indexes.

### App Store Name (30 char budget)

```
Dumpling Breath: Calm Breathing
```

- 30 characters exactly.
- Brand leads. Tail indexes **calm** + **breathing** — the two highest-volume
  terms in the category ("Indie Battlefield" per playbook §1, not the
  zero-volume "Ghost Town" of *dumpling*).
- Alternative if a colon reads badly in review: `Dumpling Breath — Breathe Calm`
  (30).

### Subtitle (30 char budget — all NEW words, zero repeats of the Name)

Primary recommendation:

```
Breathe with haptics, no account
```

(32 — trim to **`Haptic breathing, no account`** = 28, or
**`Feel each breath. No account.`** = 29.)

Candidates, all repeat-free against the Name:

| Subtitle | Chars | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| `Feel each breath. No account.` | 29 | craft + privacy (lead story) |
| `Haptic breathing, no account` | 28 | mechanic + privacy |
| `Relax, unwind, feel each breath` | 31→ trim | pure keyword slots |
| `A soft one to squeeze and slow` | 30 | character-forward |

**Chosen: `Feel each breath. No account.`** — it leads with the craft-and-privacy
story the description also leads with, and "feel" / "breath" / "account" are all
new indexed words.

### Keywords field (100 char budget, comma-separated, NO spaces, singular)

Rules applied: max the field; no word already in Name or Subtitle; no
competitor names (Apple rejects those); fight in the Indie Battlefield.

```
breathwork,anxiety,stress,panic,relax,sleep,fidget,haptic,mindful,meditation,grounding,pranayama,box,478,vagal,soothe,bedtime,focus,nervous,unwind
```

(99 chars.) Word bank / rotation pool for monthly updates — swap the weakest
ranker each release:
`calm-down, resonance, coherent, sigh, exhale, downshift, worry, overwhelm,
tension, restless, wind-down, self-soothe, nervous-system, quiet, still`

Excluded on purpose: `dumpling` (near-zero search volume — the brand already
carries it via the Name), any competitor name (Calm, Headspace, Balance,
Oak, Othership, "Haptic Calm"), any outcome/medical claim ("cure", "therapy",
"treatment").

### Promotional text (170 char, updatable without review)

```
No sign-up, no ads, no streaks, collects nothing. Just a soft dumpling you
squeeze until your breathing slows down. Now on Apple Watch, with a Lock
Screen widget.
```

## 2. Description copy

Lead with the craft and the privacy story. Not a feature list.

```
Some minutes are hard. This is a small, soft thing to hold when one of them
hits.

Press the dumpling and it fills under your thumb. Let go and it empties. Your
breath follows it without being told to — in as it swells, out as it settles.
The Taptic Engine carries the rhythm, so it works with your eyes closed and
your phone face-down on the table.

That's the whole app. One screen. One dumpling.

WHAT IT DOESN'T DO
There is no account. There is no sign-up. It never connects to the internet —
it can't, there's no networking in it at all. No ads. No analytics. No streaks,
no badges, no "you haven't breathed today" notifications. It collects nothing
about you, because there is nowhere for that to go. Put your phone in Airplane
Mode and the app is exactly as complete.

MADE TO FEEL REAL
The squeeze is a live simulation, not a video loop — it deforms under your
touch and springs back. It's tiny, it runs beautifully on an old iPhone, and it
looks identical-crisp on a 13-inch iPad and on your wrist.

EVERYWHERE YOU'D REACH FOR IT
· Apple Watch — turn the Digital Crown to set your pace; the Taptic Engine
  keeps time. Works on its own, no phone needed.
· Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets — one tap straight into a breath.
· A Control Center control and a Siri phrase — "start Dumpling Breath" — for
  the moments before you've fully surfaced.

BREATHING PATTERNS
Starts on coherent breathing (a slow, even 5.5 seconds each way) because a
stressed person shouldn't have to choose. Box breathing and 4-7-8 are one quiet
tap away if you want them.

OPTIONAL, AND OFF UNTIL YOU SAY SO
If you want it, Dumpling Breath can log a finished session to Apple Health as
Mindful Minutes, and a State of Mind entry. That's a switch you flip, it stays
on your device, and the app still has no way to send it anywhere.

Made by two people. No investors to answer to, so no reason to turn a calm
thing into a funnel.
```

Keep paragraphs short; the App Store truncates after ~3 lines before "more".
The first two sentences must carry the whole pitch.

playbook source). Real screens, big legible captions, one idea per frame. Lead
with the hero interaction — the squeeze mid-breath — **not** a welcome screen.

## 4. ASO plan — the three-part engine

### Discovery (get found)
- [ ] Naming Council + collision check (§0).
- [ ] Enter Name / Subtitle / Keywords above; push keywords to the full 100.
- [ ] Record baseline keyword ranks on launch day (expect invisible).
- [ ] Localize metadata for the launch languages (see
      `FEATURING_NOMINATION.md` — localization is a scored editorial criterion):
      start with es, fr, de, ja, zh-Hans, pt-BR.

### Conversion (win the tap)
- [ ] Icon legible at home-screen size — the mark *is* the product
      (see `APP_ICON_BRIEF.md`). Single highest-leverage featuring asset.
- [ ] 5 screenshots per the shot-list, real captured UI, hero frame first.
- [ ] Product-page A/B test after launch: screenshot order first
      (frame 2 vs frame 1 as hero), then subtitle.

### Momentum (compound with reviews)
- [ ] Native `SKStoreReviewController` / `requestReview` prompt fires **only**
      after the person completes their **3rd full breathing session** (a real
      happy peak) — once per version, never at onboarding, never after an
      interruption or error. (Cross-lane: Agent 1 owns the trigger point in the
      session code — `// TODO(agent-1)` noted in the report.)
- [ ] Reply to every review. Fix the complaint, flip the rating.
- [ ] Support-email signature: "If the dumpling helped, a short review really
      helps us stay independent."
- [ ] ~20 reviews asking for the same thing → build it, say so in a reply.
- [ ] Track keyword ranks monthly; rotate the weakest keyword each update.
